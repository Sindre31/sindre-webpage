01 — The House

A house built on cold water and quiet conviction.

Ocean Supreme AS is a Norwegian seafood house, headquartered in Aalesund — a town carved between mountain and sea. We are small enough to know every farm by name, and large enough to land salmon on three continents before sundown.

Our work begins long before the harvest. It begins with the water — its temperature, its rhythm, its reputation. Because what arrives on a plate in Osaka tomorrow began as an unbroken chain of small, deliberate decisions made today.

Provenance

Every fish is traceable to the fjord, the pen, the day.

Patience

Slow growth in cold water — never rushed, never compromised.

03 — The Journey

From a fjord at dawn to a chef's counter by sundown.

Speed is not a flourish. It is the silent margin between fresh and forgettable. Our logistics is engineered around a single number: hours since harvest.

  1. 00:00
    Harvest

    Salmon stunned, bled and chilled within minutes — the gold standard.

  2. 04:00
    Process

    Graded, trimmed and packed at +1°C in our partner facility.

  3. 10:00
    Road

    Refrigerated transit to Oslo Gardermoen — sealed, sensored, signed.

  4. 18:00
    Air freight

    Direct cargo flight, temperature-locked at 0–4°C the entire way.

  5. 36:00
    Tokyo

    Cleared, cross-docked and on a chef's counter by morning service.
