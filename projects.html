LiveQuantFinance

Meridian PMX

A Bloomberg-terminal-style exchange of portfolio-construction models, run over two universes: the 100 most-traded Oslo Børs shares benchmarked to OBX, and the 60 most-owned foreign shares on Nordnet benchmarked to the S&P 500. Ten classic models compete on real monthly history back to 2016, from CAPM and Mean-Variance to Black-Litterman, Fama-French and Hierarchical Risk Parity — each with its objective written out, tunable risk aversion and weight caps, per-holding risk contribution, and its own stated limitations.

shares-analytics.vercel.app

LiveMarketsAI

Nordlys Terminal

A Bloomberg-style terminal for the Norwegian market: live Oslo Børs and US quotes, FX, commodities and the news that moves them. Behind it runs a systematic factor portfolio — momentum, trend, low volatility, value and quality scored into one signal per stock — backtested ten years against OSEBX with Sharpe, drawdown and per-holding attribution. Illustrative, not advice.

nordlys-terminal.vercel.app
